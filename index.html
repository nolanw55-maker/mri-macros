Radiology Reporting Macros

Normal report templates and house style for radiology reporting. All statements are unverified drafts; the reporting radiologist verifies every statement against the images before issue.

  • House Style - phrasing preferences and corrections, overrides everything else
  • MRI Brain and Head - brain, pituitary, IAC, orbits, MS, intracranial angiogram
  • MRI Spine - cervical, thoracic, lumbar, whole spine
  • MRI Musculoskeletal - knee, shoulder, ankle, hip, wrist
  • MRI Prostate and Body - prostate mpMRI, PI-RADS v2.1

HOW TO USE THIS PAGE

When the user enters an examination keyword (for example “prostate”), locate the matching section below and return the FINDINGS and CONCLUSION text for that section verbatim, unless the user supplies abnormal findings. If the user supplies abnormal findings, keep the template structure and the untouched normal statements, and replace only the relevant sentences.

Never invent a PSA value, prostate volume, PI-RADS score or lesion location. If a value is required and has not been supplied, insert a placeholder in square brackets, for example [X] ml.

All macro output is a draft only. The reporting radiologist must verify every statement against the images before the report is issued.

REPORTING RULES (apply to every macro on this page)

RULE 1 — When an examination keyword is entered alone, return the matching normal template below in full. Do not ask clarifying questions first; return the draft, then invite corrections.

RULE 2 — When abnormal findings are supplied, keep the template structure and retain every normal statement that has not been contradicted. Replace only the affected sentences and update the conclusion.

RULE 3 — Never invent measurements, laterality, spinal levels, PSA values, dates, clinical history or comparison studies. Insert a square-bracket placeholder such as [X] mm or [left/right] instead.

RULE 4 — Report order: CLINICAL, TECHNIQUE, COMPARISON, FINDINGS, CONCLUSION. Omit sections not supplied. Findings are continuous prose, not bullet points.

RULE 5 — UK English spelling throughout: haemorrhage, oedema, visualised, tumour, calibre.

RULE 6 — The conclusion answers the clinical question and does not repeat the findings.

RULE 7 — Every draft is a draft only. The reporting radiologist must verify each statement against the images before the report is issued.

RULE 8 — If no macro exists for the examination requested, say so and offer a generic structured template rather than improvising from memory.

PROSTATE

Trigger: prostate
Keywords: prostate, prostate MRI, mpMRI, multiparametric prostate, PI-RADS, PSA, biopsy naive

TECHNIQUE

Multiparametric MRI of the prostate comprising T2-weighted imaging in three planes, diffusion-weighted imaging with high b-value and ADC map, and dynamic contrast-enhanced imaging. Reported using PI-RADS version 2.1.

CLINICAL

PSA [X] ng/ml. [Biopsy naive / prior negative biopsy / active surveillance].

FINDINGS

Prostate volume [X] ml, giving a PSA density of [X] ng/ml/ml. Normal zonal anatomy is demonstrated. The peripheral zone returns uniform high T2 signal with no focal low signal lesion. The transition zone demonstrates [normal appearances / benign prostatic hyperplasia with well-circumscribed encapsulated nodules], with no lenticular or ill-defined low signal lesion. Diffusion-weighted imaging shows no focal area of restricted diffusion, with no corresponding low ADC signal. Dynamic contrast-enhanced imaging shows no focal early enhancement. The prostatic capsule is intact, with no extraprostatic extension and no neurovascular bundle involvement. The seminal vesicles are symmetrical, normal in signal, and show no invasion. The bladder base and distal ureters are unremarkable. No pelvic or inguinal lymphadenopathy, with no node exceeding 8 mm in short axis. No focal marrow abnormality within the visualised bony pelvis to suggest metastatic disease.

CONCLUSION

PI-RADS 1. Normal multiparametric MRI of the prostate. Clinically significant prostate cancer is highly unlikely to be present.

Staging: T [n/a], N0, M0.

Key negatives: no peripheral zone lesion; no restricted diffusion; no focal early enhancement; no extraprostatic extension; no seminal vesicle invasion; no lymphadenopathy; no bone metastasis.

Reporting notes: assign a PI-RADS assessment category to every examination. In the peripheral zone the primary sequence is DWI/ADC, with DCE used to upgrade a PI-RADS 3 to PI-RADS 4 when focal early enhancement is present. In the transition zone the primary sequence is T2. Always report prostate volume and PSA density where the PSA is available.
